# Delete your Caffeine Health data

**App:** Caffeine Health · **Developer:** Health. · **Contact:** healthdot.support@gmail.com

Caffeine Health has no accounts and no developer server. Your data is stored on your phone, and
optionally in your own Google Drive, so you can delete almost all of it yourself, immediately,
without contacting anyone.

## How to delete your data

### 1. Data on your phone (drink history, custom drinks, health profile, settings)

- **Delete everything:** open your phone's **Settings → Apps → Caffeine Health → Storage → Clear
  storage**, **or** uninstall the app. Either removes everything the app stored on the phone.
- **Delete single entries:** on Home, swipe a drink to the left and tap **Delete**.

Deleted immediately. Nothing is kept, and the developer never had a copy.

### 2. Google Drive backup (Google Play edition, only if you turned it on)

- In the app: **Settings → My data → Delete backup from Google Drive**. This also stops automatic
  backups.
- Or in Google Drive on the web: **Settings → Manage apps → Caffeine Health → Delete hidden app
  data**. To remove the app's access as well, open your Google Account → **Security → Third-party
  apps with account access → Caffeine Health → Remove access**.

Deleted immediately from your Drive. The developer cannot access this backup.

### 3. Health Connect (only if you turned it on)

Drinks the app wrote to Health Connect stay there until you delete them: open **Health Connect →
App permissions / Data → Caffeine Health** and delete its data, then revoke its permissions.

### 4. Caffeine Health+ purchase records (Google Play edition, only if you bought Plus)

Your subscription or Lifetime purchase is verified by RevenueCat with a random, anonymous ID (no
name or email). To have these purchase records deleted, email **healthdot.support@gmail.com** with the subject
"Delete my purchase data" and, if you can, the date and plan you bought. They are deleted within
30 days.

Deleting purchase records does not cancel a subscription; cancel it in Google Play first
(**Play Store → Profile → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions**). Google keeps its own order
and payment records as required by law, under the
[Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

## What is kept

| Data | After you delete |
|---|---|
| Drink history, custom drinks, health profile, settings (phone) | Nothing kept |
| Google Drive backup | Nothing kept |
| Health Connect entries | Kept in Health Connect until you delete them there |
| RevenueCat purchase records | Deleted within 30 days of your email request |
| Google Play order and payment records | Kept by Google under its legal obligations |

More detail: [Caffeine Health privacy policy](PRIVACY.md).
